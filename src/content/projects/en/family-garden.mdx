## The brief

**Family Garden** is a licensed care home for the elderly in **Pančevo, Serbia**, with **42 places**, **medical care around the clock** and five meals a day. It offers permanent and short-term stays and care for residents with dementia and Alzheimer's disease.

The people who visit a care home's website are usually not the future residents. They are sons and daughters who need to find good care for a parent, often in a hurry. They want three answers fast: is the home licensed, what care does it offer, and how do I reach someone today.

## What I built

- **A fast Astro home page** that loads in a fraction of a second on a phone, because most families search on their phone.
- **Clear facts at the top**: licence, number of places, 24/7 medical care and the types of stay on offer.
- **A short section for each kind of care**, from permanent stays to short stays and dementia care, in plain language for families.
- **Phone and contact always in reach**, so a family member can call without searching the page.
- **Local search basics**: the right title and description, the address in Pančevo and structured data, so the home shows up when families search for a care home in the area.

## The result

Family Garden now has a modern, fast front page that answers families' first questions and makes the first call easy.
