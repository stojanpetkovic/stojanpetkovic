## The brief

**Family Garden** is a licensed care home for the elderly in **Pančevo, Serbia**, with **42 places**, **medical care around the clock** and five meals a day. It offers permanent and short-term stays and care for residents with dementia and Alzheimer's disease.

The people who visit a care home's website are usually not the future residents. They are sons and daughters who need to find good care for a parent, often in a hurry. They want three answers fast: is the home licensed, what care does it offer, and how do I reach someone today.

## What I built

- **A fast Astro website** with four main pages: home, care and services, rooms, and contact. It loads in a fraction of a second on a phone, because most families search on their phone.
- **Clear facts at the top**: licence, 42 places, 24/7 medical care, five meals a day and the licence end date.
- **A care page** that explains each kind of care in plain language, from everyday help to dementia, Alzheimer's and recovery after a stroke, with its own FAQ.
- **A rooms page** with single, double and multi-bed rooms and apartments, a photo gallery and a park view.
- **Phone and enquiry form always in reach**: click-to-call in the header and hero, and a short form that asks for the type of stay and the resident's mobility.
- **Local SEO**: titles and descriptions for searches like "dom za stare Pančevo", the address next to Goranski park, a privacy-friendly map and structured data for the care home, its licence, services and FAQ.
- **Lead tracking**, so every call and enquiry from the site is counted.

## The result

Family Garden now has a modern, fast website that answers families' first questions and makes the first call easy.
