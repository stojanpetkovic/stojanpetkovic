## The brief

**Erić Dental Centar** is a dental practice in Pančevo, Serbia, opened in 2020 at Oslobođenja 23. Its patients come from Pančevo and from Belgrade. The old site was a WordPress theme with demo content that had never been removed: the theme's sample doctors, an empty "Announcements" section and pages that had nothing to do with the practice. Above all, **there was no clear path from a search to a booked appointment**.

The goal for the new site: a patient looking for a dentist in Pančevo finds the practice, understands what it does and sends an appointment request in under a minute, in their own language.

## What I built

- **A new 60-page Astro website** in Serbian, English and German, with every page translated, not machine-copied.
- **A page for each treatment**, so patients find the practice when they search for implants, prosthetics, orthodontics or aesthetic dentistry in Pančevo.
- **An online booking request form** in every language, and click-to-call on phones.
- **Lead tracking**, so the practice sees how many patients found it online and how they got in touch.
- **Real photos of the practice and team**, replacing the theme's demo doctors and empty sections.

## Built for search

A dental practice competes in a small area, so local SEO decides who gets the patient. The site uses the practice's exact name, address on Oslobođenja and phone everywhere, matching the Google Business Profile. Each treatment page targets searches like "dentist Pančevo" and "dental implants Pančevo", and hreflang tags tell Google which language version to show. Patients from Belgrade and from abroad find the English and German pages.
