## The brief

**Erić Dental Centar** is a dental practice in Pančevo, Serbia, opened in 2020 at Oslobođenja 23. Its patients come from Pančevo and from Belgrade. The old site was a WordPress theme with demo content that had never been removed: the theme's sample doctors, an empty "Announcements" section and pages that had nothing to do with the practice. Above all, **there was no clear path from a search to a booked appointment**.

The goal for the new site: a patient looking for a dentist in Pančevo finds the practice, understands what it does and sends an appointment request in under a minute, in their own language.

## A site that sells the service

The site was built from scratch with **Astro and Tailwind CSS** and ships as static HTML, with no framework loading in the browser. The design is built around trust:

- **A large hero** with a photo of the owner, Dr Miloš Erić, the **5.0 rating from 31 patient reviews** on doktor.rs and a clear booking button.
- **Custom icons for all 9 services** and the logos of the implant brands the practice uses: Straumann, Zimmer and IRES.
- Sections for the practice, the team, patient reviews and the blog, in the practice's colours.
- On phones, **"Call" and "Book an appointment"** buttons stay within thumb's reach.

## Booking that converts

The contact page works as a **landing page**: the form is on the first screen, only name and phone are required, and the service and preferred time are picked with a single tap. From every service page the form opens **with that service already selected**, and every page ends with a call to book.

Every request goes into my lead-tracking system. The practice is notified immediately, and each request shows **the page the patient came from, the service they want and the language they wrote in**. Visits and phone clicks are counted too, without cookies, and all of it goes into a monthly report.

## Local SEO

- **A page for every service**, written for what people actually search for ("dental implants Pančevo", "children's dentist Pančevo"), with FAQs and related articles.
- **Structured data** describes the practice as a dentist with its address, GPS coordinates, opening hours, service area (Pančevo, the South Banat District, Belgrade), a catalogue of all 9 services and the team.
- The site is linked to the **Google Business Profile**, social profiles and the doktor.rs listing, with the same name, address and phone everywhere.
- Service pages and articles are marked up as **medical content**, with Dr Miloš Erić as the author. For health topics, Google looks closely at who stands behind the content.
- **No old URL was lost.** Services and articles kept the URLs they had on WordPress, and the nine remaining WordPress URLs permanently redirect to the new pages.

## Three languages

The entire site is translated into **English and German**: all 9 services, the blog, the form including its error messages, the FAQs and the privacy policy. That makes **60 pages, 20 in each language**.

- Each language has **URLs in that language**, such as `/en/dental-implants/` and `/de/zahnimplantate/`, so the pages can rank in English and German search.
- **Hreflang tags** tell Google which page translates which, so a visitor from Germany gets the German version while Serbian stays the default.
- The language switcher leads to **the same page** in the other language. Foreign visitors see the phone number in international format, and patient reviews are marked as translated from Serbian.

For a practice that treats patients from abroad, this is an open channel to the diaspora and international patients.
