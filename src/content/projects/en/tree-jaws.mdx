## The brief

**Tree Jaws** is a tree removal, trimming, and stump-grinding company serving residential and commercial properties across **South Florida**. Trust and speed decide who gets the call in this business — a homeowner with a storm-damaged tree isn't comparison-shopping, they're picking the first company that looks credible and answers the phone. The site and the marketing around it needed to do that convincing in the first few seconds, then keep doing it every month after.

I work with Tree Jaws on all three legs of that: the site itself, the SEO underneath it, and the Google Ads account running alongside it.
