## The brief

**Tree Jaws** is a tree removal, trimming, and stump-grinding company serving residential and commercial properties across **South Florida**. Trust and speed decide who gets the call in this business — a homeowner with a storm-damaged tree isn't comparison-shopping, they're picking the first company that looks credible and answers the phone. The site and the marketing around it needed to do that convincing in the first few seconds, then keep doing it every month after.

I work with Tree Jaws on all three legs of that: the site itself, the SEO underneath it, and the Google Ads account running alongside it.

## What I built

- **A fast Astro website** for a tree service in South Florida, with a page for each core service: tree removal, tree trimming and stump grinding, for homes and commercial properties.
- **Calls first on every page.** The phone number, a free quote form and a click-to-call button are always in reach, because a homeowner with a fallen tree wants to talk to someone now.
- **Trust signals up front**: the 4.9★ rating from 427 Google reviews, licensing and real photos of the crew at work.
- **Google Ads for search and Maps**, built around high-intent searches like "tree removal near me" and "emergency tree service", with negative keywords that cut out DIY and job searches.
- **Conversion tracking for every lead type**: phone calls, lead forms, contact forms and requests for directions, so the client sees what each lead costs.

## Built for search

Tree service is a local business, so the site is built for local search. Each service page has its own title, description and copy written for South Florida homeowners, and structured data tells Google what the company does and where. The site is linked to the Google Business Profile, and ongoing SEO keeps adding content and fixing what Search Console reports. Ads bring calls today; SEO brings more of them every month without paying per click.
