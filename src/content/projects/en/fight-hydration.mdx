## The brief

**Fight Hydration** sells one product: a lemon-flavoured drink mix with **5 g of creatine monohydrate and 1,260 mg of electrolytes, with no sugar**. It was developed with professional boxer **Filip Stanković** in Florida and sells to customers across the United States, in English.

A brand with a single product doesn't need a big catalogue. It needs one page that explains the product clearly, answers the questions people have before they buy a supplement, and makes checkout easy. That was the brief.

## What I built

- **A Shopify store** set up around one product, so every page leads to the same buy button.
- **A product page** with the facts buyers look for first: what is in each serving, how many servings are in a tub, the price and how to use it.
- **Subscriptions**, so regular customers can get a new tub every month without reordering.
- **An FAQ section** that answers common questions about creatine, electrolytes and sugar before a customer has to email.
- **A 30-day guarantee** shown near the buy button, to make the first order an easy decision.
- **A wholesale section** for gyms and shops that want to stock the product.
- **Video and an Instagram feed** with the athlete behind the brand, so visitors see real training, not stock photos.

## Why Shopify

For a store that has to take payments, handle subscriptions and ship across the US, Shopify was the right tool. The owner can manage orders, stock and discounts without a developer, and payments and taxes are handled by the platform.
