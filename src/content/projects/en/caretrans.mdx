## The brief

**CARETRANS** is a medical transport company from Belgrade, available **24/7, every day of the year**. It transports bedridden and less mobile patients across Serbia, to the country's spas and brings patients home from Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Italy.

The old site ran on **WordPress** and had **only a few plain pages**: no page per service, no content to rank for, and a slow load on the phones most people use when they urgently need transport. When someone needs an ambulance, they call **the first company whose site opens and tells them what they need to know**. The goal: a faster site that ranks for every service and location the company covers, and turns visits into calls.
