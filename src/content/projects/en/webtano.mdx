## What Webtano is

**Webtano** ([webtano.com](https://webtano.com)) is the platform I built and run for my clients. Every website I make sits on top of it. A client doesn't get "just a website": they get a site, a system that catches every enquiry and call, and one clear report a month that shows what their marketing money brought in.

I built it because small businesses kept asking the same question: *which of the money I spend actually brings customers?* Ad platforms report clicks and impressions. Webtano reports enquiries, calls and the cost of each one, by source.

## What it does

- **Fast websites for local businesses.** Custom design, a page for every service and location, the phone number on the first screen, and Google speed scores of 90+.
- **A lead system on every client site.** One small script (`leads.js`) counts page views without cookies, catches every form, and counts taps on the phone number, WhatsApp, SMS and email as enquiries.
- **Instant lead alerts.** The full message, the page it came from and the ad or search behind it reach the client's inbox the moment it is sent.
- **Source for every enquiry.** Each visit and lead is sorted into a channel the client can read: Google Ads, Google Search, Google Business Profile, AI search, Bing, Facebook and Instagram ads, other social and other websites.
- **Monthly client reports.** Visitors, enquiries, calls, conversion rate, most visited pages, devices, countries and every enquiry in the period, printable as a PDF.
- **Cost per enquiry and ROAS.** Add the ad spend and the report shows what each enquiry really cost and what it earned.
- **Google Ads calls and AI search.** Calls from call ads that never touch the site are imported from Google Ads, and Search Console data shows how often the site appears in Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode.
- **CRM and finance behind it.** Leads, customers, projects, proposals with PDF, tasks, a price list, automations such as a morning digest and a reminder for unanswered leads, plus invoices, income and expenses.

## Why it matters for clients

Most small business websites look fine and still lose enquiries in places the owner never sees. Webtano closes those gaps: the site loads fast and shows the number, every enquiry reaches the owner at once, and the monthly report answers the only question that matters. Domain, code, hosting and ad accounts stay in the client's name from day one.
