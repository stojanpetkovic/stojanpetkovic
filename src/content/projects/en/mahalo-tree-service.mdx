## The brief

**Mahalo Tree Service** is a family-owned tree removal and trimming company serving South Florida, built around bonded, **ISA Certified arborists** and a **5.0 Google rating**. This one came in narrower in scope than most of my client work: no ad campaign to run, no funnel to design — just a site that was holding the business back, and a search presence that needed real content behind it rather than thin, duplicated pages.

## What I built

- **A rebuild from WordPress to Astro**, so pages load fast on phones and there is no plugin stack to update or secure.
- **18 service area pages**, one for each city the company serves in South Florida, each with its own copy instead of the same text with a new city name.
- **A 24-post blog** that answers real homeowner questions about tree care, storm prep and when a tree needs to come down.
- **Instant lead alerts**, so the owner gets every quote request the moment it is sent and can call back first.
- **Trust on every page**: the 5.0 Google rating, bonded crews and ISA Certified arborists.

## Built for search

Local SEO for a tree service comes down to two things: a page that matches what people search in each city, and proof that the company is real and trusted. The area pages target searches like "tree removal in [city]", the blog builds topical authority for tree care, and internal links connect every post to the right service and area page. Titles, descriptions, structured data and a clean sitemap help Google index all of it.
