## The brief

**Valena Apartmani** rents two apartments on **Divčibare**, a mountain resort in western Serbia, to guests booking a weekend or week away near the ski slopes and hiking trails. Unlike the tree-service work in this portfolio, this wasn't a rebuild — there was no existing site to replace, no SEO history to protect. The brief was to get a fast, credible booking site live from nothing, and drive guests to it directly with paid social, since that's where this kind of short-term-rental audience in Serbia actually books from.
