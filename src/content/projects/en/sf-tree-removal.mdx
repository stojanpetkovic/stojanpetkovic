## The brief

**SF Tree Removal** offers tree removal, emergency storm response and certified arborist work across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach County. The domain had been running a heavy, app-like build that scored 69 for performance. The goal of the rebuild was simple: **a site that does one job well**. A homeowner searching for tree removal in their city should land on a page written for that city, understand what the company does, and send a request in under a minute.

## What I built

- **A static Astro and Tailwind CSS site** in place of a heavy app-like build, taking Lighthouse performance from 69 to 97.
- **A page for each service**: tree removal, emergency storm response and certified arborist work.
- **City pages for Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach County**, so a homeowner lands on a page written for their area.
- **Form-only contact**, as the client wanted, with a short estimate request that takes under a minute.
- **Lead counting**: every visit and every request shows up in the client's report.

## Built for search

Google ranks pages, not websites, so a tree removal company needs a page for every service and every area it wants to be found in. Each page has its own title, meta description, headings and structured data, and links to the related services and cities. Fast loading on phones helps both rankings and conversions, which is why speed was the first thing fixed.
