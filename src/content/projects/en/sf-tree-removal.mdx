## The brief

**SF Tree Removal** offers tree removal, emergency storm response and certified arborist work across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach County. The domain had been running a heavy, app-like build that scored 69 for performance. The goal of the rebuild was simple: **a site that does one job well**. A homeowner searching for tree removal in their city should land on a page written for that city, understand what the company does, and send a request in under a minute.
