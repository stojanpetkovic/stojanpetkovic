## The brief

**koznaobuca.com** was a single-page shop selling one model of leather shoes through an order form. Search Console showed something the shop wasn't using: people were finding the domain for **general searches about leather footwear** – leather shoes, chukka-style "spenserice", shoe sizes in centimetres, women's boots.

The client wanted to **stop selling for now and turn the domain into a blog** that answers exactly those searches. The requirements:

- a professional blog look, built on a ready-made open-source theme the client chose;
- around **20 articles in Serbian** on general leather footwear keywords, **without targeting brand names**;
- content based on reputable English-language sources, with stock photos;
- **contact only through a form**, no phone number on the site;
- a fast, static site that runs on the existing **cPanel hosting**.

## 20 articles from reputable sources

I took the keywords straight from the client's Search Console export and kept only the general ones. Each article was **written from scratch in Serbian**, based on advice from respected English-language footwear and foot-health publications, and lists those sources at the end.

The articles are split into three topics:

- **Guides**: how to recognise quality leather, measuring your foot and shoe size in cm, leather types, Goodyear-welted vs cemented soles, leather vs rubber soles, domestic leather footwear.
- **Style**: chukka boots for men and women, Chelsea boots, women's boots and over-the-knee boots, sandals and huaraches, men's shoe types, women's shoes for work.
- **Care**: cleaning and conditioning, winter protection from salt and snow, scuffs and stains, shoe odour, leather sneakers.

Every article has a **FAQ** answering real questions, internal links to related articles and a stock photo credited under the image.

## From a shop to a blog, without losing traffic

- **Every address Google already knew still works.** The order page, the thank-you page, the purchase terms and the old shop's product URLs permanently redirect to the most relevant new page; the size chart redirects to the article that now contains it.
- The site is built in **Astro** on the open-source **ProCleaning** theme, adapted to the brand, and ships as **static HTML** on the client's cPanel hosting.
- **Contact goes through one form** on the contact page, sent by a small PHP script with spam protection. Other pages lead there with a call-to-action button.

## SEO built in

- **Article, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data** on every post, with the sources listed as citations.
- An **image sitemap**, RSS, `llms.txt` for AI search and clean, one-heading pages.
- **Search** that works without a server.
- Analytics and the Meta Pixel load on the first interaction, so they never slow the first paint.

## Tracking and a banner

Every page runs my **lead tracking**, so the client sees every form submission. A **square banner** in the homepage hero and a **popup after 40 seconds of reading** promote web design services, with UTM tags so each placement can be measured.
