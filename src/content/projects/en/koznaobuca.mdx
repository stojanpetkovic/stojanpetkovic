## The brief

**koznaobuca.com** was a single-page shop selling one model of leather shoes through an order form. Search Console showed something the shop wasn't using: people were finding the domain for **general searches about leather footwear** – leather shoes, chukka-style "spenserice", shoe sizes in centimetres, women's boots.

The client wanted to **stop selling for now and turn the domain into a blog** that answers exactly those searches. The requirements:

- a professional blog look, built on a ready-made open-source theme the client chose;
- around **20 articles in Serbian** on general leather footwear keywords, **without targeting brand names**;
- content based on reputable English-language sources, with stock photos;
- **contact only through a form**, no phone number on the site;
- a fast, static site that runs on the existing **cPanel hosting**.

## What I built

- **A blog in place of a one-product shop**, using the domain's existing search visibility for leather footwear.
- **20 researched articles in Serbian** on general topics people search for: leather shoes, chukka boots, shoe sizes in centimetres, women's boots and leather care.
- **A professional blog design** on the open-source theme the client chose, with stock photos.
- **Every old URL kept or redirected**, so nothing the domain had built up was lost.
- **A fast static site on cPanel**, with contact only through a form, as the client wanted.

## Built for search

Search Console showed which questions already brought people to the domain, so the content plan started there. Each article targets one clear topic, without brand names, and is based on reputable sources. Titles, descriptions, headings and internal links tie the articles together into a topic cluster on leather footwear, and the site scores 100 in every Lighthouse category.
